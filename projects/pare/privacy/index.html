Pare Studio Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Pare Studio and Asariko ("we", "our", or "us") handle information when you use the Pare Studio app for Mac. On your Mac, the app appears as “Pare”.

The Short Version

Pare Studio works entirely on your Mac. It does not collect any data, does not connect to the internet, and does not send anything anywhere.

Your Photos and Videos

The files you give Pare Studio are processed on your Mac only. They are never uploaded. Pare Studio saves the smaller copies where you choose, next to the original or in a folder you picked, and nowhere else. Your originals are never overwritten. With “Replace original”, the original is moved to the Trash, where you can still get it back.

Location Data

“Remove location data” is on by default: Pare Studio removes the location from the copies it makes. You can turn it off in Settings. Your originals keep their location.

Folder Permissions

The first time Pare Studio saves into a folder, it asks for your permission. Your answer is stored only on your Mac, in the app’s own storage, so it doesn’t ask again.

Settings and Logs

Your settings are stored only on your Mac. Pare Studio can write a troubleshooting log, which is off by default. The log stays on your Mac and is never sent to us.

No Tracking

Pare Studio has no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party services. The open-source tools inside Pare Studio (libwebp, oxipng and libjpeg-turbo) also run only on your Mac.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data, because Pare Studio does not collect any. Pare Studio is downloaded from the Mac App Store, so the download is handled by Apple under Apple’s own privacy policy. We do not receive your personal information from Apple; we only see anonymous totals, such as the number of downloads.

Data Security

Pare Studio is designed with privacy and data minimization in mind: your files are processed on your Mac and never leave it, and we store nothing on our side. Your files are protected by your Mac’s own security, such as your login password and FileVault.

International Use

Pare Studio works the same way in every country. Because it does not collect or send any data, no personal data is transferred across borders. Local laws on privacy and data rights may still differ where you live.

Children’s Privacy

Pare Studio collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Future Changes

Pare Studio does not use third-party analytics or data tracking tools, and it does not connect to the internet. If a future version adds a feature that needs any of these, this Privacy Policy will be updated before that version is released.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we do, we will post the updated version and revise the “Last updated” date above. Continued use of Pare Studio after changes become effective means you accept the revised policy.
