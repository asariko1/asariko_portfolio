How it works

  1. 1Open a spreadsheetAn Excel (.xlsx, .xls) or CSV file. It is read in your browser, never uploaded.
  2. 2Pick the sheet and columnThe header row is found automatically, and you can change it if it guessed wrong.
  3. 3Start typingEvery match lines up in a clean list, filtered as you type.

Why use it

Tired of pressing Enter over and over in Excel’s Find, watching the screen jump from cell to cell until you lose track of where you started? Type a few letters instead, and every match lines up in one list you can scan at a glance.

  • Search any sheet and any column
  • Header row detected automatically
  • Results update as you type
  • Free and open source; nothing is uploaded
