Pare PDF

Draw one box, crop every page.

Open a PDF, drag a box around the part you need, and Pare PDF crops that same spot from every page you pick. It also combines PDFs in the order you set.

How it works

  1. 1Open a PDFDrop it on the window or the Dock icon, or use Open With. Every page shows at once.
  2. 2Draw one boxDrag around a chart, a stamp or a header. A blue copy shows on every page that will be cropped.
  3. 3Save CropYou get one new PDF, or a .zip with a file per page, saved next to the original.

Combine PDFs

Add two or more PDFs and put them in order. Drag the cards in the app, or use the arrows. Save Combine makes one file. Send to Crop opens the result in Crop, so you can crop across all of them.

Try it: move or remove a card, or press Send to Crop.

Mini mode

Shrink the window to a small square that stays on top of your other windows. Drop one PDF on it to crop it, or several to combine them. Keep the full window on top with the pin.

What it does

Pick the pages

Only the page you drew on, all pages, odd or even pages. Narrow it further with a list like 1-10,15.

Text stays text

The crop changes the page’s box, it doesn’t turn the page into a picture. Text stays selectable and fonts stay embedded.

One PDF or separate files

Save the crops as one PDF, or as a .zip with one PDF per page. Full page mode exports pages as they are.

Never overwrites

Files are saved next to the original or in a folder you choose. An existing file gets a new name, like report_cropped 2.pdf.

On your Mac, nothing uploaded

Pare PDF uses Apple’s built-in PDFKit, on your Mac. Your files are never uploaded, and there is no account to make. Prefer the browser? PDF Crop does the same, free and also without uploading anything.
