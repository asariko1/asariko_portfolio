Pare Social

Try it: tick or untick sizes. These are the app’s real crops of three photos.

How it works

  1. 1Tick your sizesPick the sizes you post at from the sidebar, grouped by platform. Your ticks are remembered.
  2. 2Drop photos or videosEach file gets one card per ticked size, cropped around the faces or the main subject.
  3. 3Every size is savedFiles are saved right away, named like photo-ig-story.jpg, ready to upload.

Crops around faces

Pare Social finds the faces in a photo and keeps them in every size, from a tall Story to a wide thumbnail.

No faces? It looks for the most eye-catching part of the picture instead. It all happens on your Mac with Apple Vision, and face positions are thrown away once the crop is placed.

Videos follow the face

Turning a wide video into a Story or Reel? Pare Social finds the face every 0.2 seconds and moves the crop with it. When the face isn’t seen for a moment, the frame holds still.

Adjust any crop

The automatic crop is usually right. When it isn’t, double-click a card and move it yourself.

All together shows every size as a coloured frame over the photo; drag the photo under them. One by one lets you drag and zoom inside a single frame, then use that spot for all sizes.

Instagram profile grid

Split one photo into 3, 6 or 9 posts that line up on your profile.

Tiles are 3:4, the shape the profile grid shows, and numbered in posting order: the last tile goes up first, so the picture comes out right.

What it does

Photos and videos

Videos get one fixed crop, chosen from the first seconds, which you can move.

Ready to upload

Photos are saved as JPEG in standard colours, videos as MP4 (H.264, 1080p, up to 60 fps, audio kept). Pare Studio’s compression keeps them light.

Location removed

GPS location is taken out by default. The capture date stays.

Saved where you want

In one folder you choose, with a subfolder for each drop, or next to the originals.

Changed your ticks?

Tick new sizes after a drop and one click makes them for the files already in the list.

Mini mode

Shrink the window to a small square that stays on top. Drop files on it and they’re saved at your ticked sizes.

Every size it makes

23 sizes in six groups. New sizes arrive with app updates.

On your Mac, nothing uploaded

Pare Social finds faces and subjects with Apple’s built-in Vision framework, on your Mac. Face positions are used only to place the crop, then thrown away. Nothing is recognised, stored or sent anywhere, and there is no account to make.
