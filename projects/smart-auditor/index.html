How it works

  1. 1Add your Gemini keyPaste a key from Google AI Studio. It is never saved and clears when you close the tab.
  2. 2Upload two filesThe AEM schedule (.xlsx or .csv), plus the Compass PDF page or a TESS export.
  3. 3Run the auditFollow each step in the live console, then read the report.

Why use it

Smart Auditor cross-references an AEM schedule against a source of truth, either the Cruise Compass PDF or a TESS report, and uses Google Gemini to spot mismatches. It understands venue synonyms and loose title variants, while requiring times to match exactly.

Your files are read in your browser and never uploaded. The text extracted from them is sent directly to Google’s Gemini API for the comparison, so check Google’s API terms before using it with confidential schedules.

  • Two sources: Compass PDF or TESS export
  • Understands venue synonyms, like Pool Deck and Poolside
  • Results grouped into missing, extra, time, venue and duplicates
  • Live console shows each step as it runs
